What is Sen Linguistics?

Language is full of meaning that depends on context, culture, and careful judgment, and this is exactly the kind of thing that AI tends to get wrong because it works from what is most probable rather than from a real understanding of the particular case. Sen Linguistics exists to do the work where those details genuinely matter, and where getting them wrong can carry a real cost. I focus on two areas in particular:

  • Translation for bringing complex, context-dependent information from Japanese and Chinese into clear, accurate English
  • Software for building custom systems that help you work with linguistic data in ways you can actually trust

Why Me?

I'm a scientist

Not all translators come with first-hand experience in research. My background in scientific research has given me insight into how complex systems work, and just as importantly, it has made me unafraid of the deep research that difficult material demands, whether that means understanding a technical process or piecing together the context behind an old document.

I have in-house experience

I've completed rigorous training in translation at the World Intellectual Property Organization (WIPO), gaining an understanding of technical language in a wide range of fields and an eye for handling high-stakes content.

I have a linguist's understanding

My training as a linguist means I pay close attention to how language actually works, from its structure and meaning to the way it shifts across subjects, time, and cultures. Rather than settling for whatever wording seems most probable, which is where machine translation tends to go wrong, I make choices by asking deeper questions: what did the writer actually intend, who were they writing for, and what context did they take for granted?

Software

← Back to Services

A growing amount of important work now depends on software that has to deal with language, whether that means making sense of large collections of documents, checking the quality of writing, or using AI without being misled by it. This is difficult software to build well because meaning depends on context and human judgment, and a system that looks correct can still be quietly wrong. I build custom systems for this kind of work, drawing on a background in both linguistics and computer science, focusing on the situations where a general-purpose AI tool cannot be trusted on its own.

Who Can Benefit

  • Businesses bringing AI into their work who want to be confident it is reliable before coming to depend on it
  • Organizations holding large amounts of documents or data who need a better way to search, organize, and make sense of it
  • Teams that need to trust the accuracy of the text they depend on, whether it is written or translated by people or by AI

Not sure? Feel free to just ask me.
